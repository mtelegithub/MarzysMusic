Sound Sensitivity

Sound is my passion. I have always been fascinated with sounds. Initially this fascination was intuitive, however it later led me to investigate audio resonance and spectral tuning in a PhD thesis. During these enlightening years I have produced music and sound design for multimedia, screen and radio. I have assisted various clients including: academics, performing artists and film directors by creating works that include instrumental arrangements, electronic synthesis and full soundtrack production. Let me create a striking soundscape that carries your idea to its ultimate vision.
